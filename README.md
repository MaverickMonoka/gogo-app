# GoGo

Mobile-first local commerce platform for Groblersdal and surrounding communities.

## Initial launch focus

1. **Groceries** — Moratiwa SUPERSPAR storefront with catalogue specials, large clean product imagery, search, categories, basket, delivery/collection and checkout.
2. **Stays** — lodge and guest-house onboarding, property listings, rooms, amenities, enquiries/bookings and location information.

## Product principles

- GoGo is the parent experience; Moratiwa SUPERSPAR is a grocery merchant inside GoGo.
- Mobile-first UX.
- Product images must be prominent and independently visible, not tiny catalogue thumbnails.
- Shared GoGo customer account for shopping and stays.
- Merchant onboarding and management are part of the platform.
- Other retailer categories follow after Groceries and Stays are working well.
